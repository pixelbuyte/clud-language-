## Roots

Three or more letters, always ending in a consonant. Bare, a root names a thing.
Add one ending to give it a job: `-a` doing (now), `-i` did, `-o` will do,
`-u` would do, `-e` describing. Example: `kod` code, `koda` codes, `kodi` coded.
